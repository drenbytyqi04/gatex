Our Services

At GATEX, we go beyond manufacturing—delivering a full suite of product-related services
designed to support your business at every stage.

Fiberglass Mesh Manufacturing

Built around the process we know best—warping, weaving, coating and finishing reinforcement mesh with consistency from one production run to the next.

ETICS & Thermal Reinforcement Systems

Our mesh strengthens ETICS systems by preventing cracks, stress, and delamination. It’s compatible with all major adhesives, profiles, and insulation boards ensuring long-term façade durability.

Private Label & OEM Production

We offer private-label fiberglass mesh with custom colours, packaging, multilingual labelling and customer-specific branding. Distributors can scale their product range with GATEX-manufactured reinforcement mesh supported by applicable technical documentation and consistent production quality.

Logistics & Consolidated Delivery

From finished roll to loaded truck, we coordinate packing, palletisation and delivery around the way our customers actually buy, stock and distribute.

Technical Documentation & Certification Support

Qualifying GATEX reinforcement mesh products are supported by applicable technical and regulatory documentation, including European Technical Assessment, corresponding Declarations of Performance and CE marking.

Color-Coded Mesh Customization

More than a colour choice—custom mesh colours become part of product identity, helping distinguish specifications, organise ranges and strengthen customer branding.
