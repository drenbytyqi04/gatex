Technical Specifications

The connection window profile should be installed after mounting the thermal insulation boards and before applying façade adhesives and base mesh layers. Installation steps:

Step 1
Wipe window frame surfaces with a damp cloth to remove dust or debris. Allow to dry.

Step 2
Remove the liner from the PE foam tape and press the profile firmly onto the frame across its full length.

Step 3
Remove the protective film from the double-sided adhesive tape and apply the protective foil to cover the entire window surface.

Step 4
Apply façade adhesive and embed the fiberglass mesh of the profile into the wet layer. Continue with the remaining façade system layers.

Step 5
After all façade work is completed, remove the protective foil along with the detachable upper edge of the profile.

Installation temperature: +5°C to +35°C
Installation must be performed by qualified or trained personnel.
