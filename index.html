Our Services
At GATEX, innovation is a continuous process built around the manufacturing of high-performance fiberglass reinforcement mesh. Our in-house manufacturing process covers warping, weaving, coating/impregnation, drying/curing, finishing and final inspection, ensuring consistent quality and full traceability. With approximately 4.5 million square meters of fiberglass mesh produced during the reference period, GATEX serves insulation system manufacturers and construction distributors across European markets. Our production facility combines modern machinery, precision coating technology and alkali-resistant coating formulations designed to support long-term durability and reliable performance under demanding conditions.

ETICS & Thermal Reinforcement Systems
