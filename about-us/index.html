What We Do

We are a European manufacturer of high-performance fiberglass reinforcement mesh for ETICS, façade reinforcement and thermal insulation applications. Based in a region with a strong textile heritage, GATEX operates a fully integrated production line — from warping and weaving to impregnation and finishing — ensuring complete quality control and traceability. Qualifying GATEX reinforcement mesh products are ETA-assessed and CE-marked, while GATEX operates certified ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 management systems. Designed for durability and easy installation, GATEX fiberglass mesh is compatible with major adhesives, corner profiles and base coats — a reliable reinforcement solution for both new construction and renovation projects across Europe.

ETA Certified Excellence
/

We are a trusted manufacturer whose reinforcement mesh systems are rigorously tested and certified under the European Technical Assessment (ETA) — EAD 040016-01-0404. Each product is developed, examined, and approved in accordance with CE Marking, ETAG 004, and TÜV Nord standards, ensuring performance, reliability, and full compliance. From Kaçanik, in the Western Balkans, we uphold a culture of engineering precision — where consistency, quality, and care define every roll we produce.

Who We Are
/

We are a dedicated team of engineers, technicians and production specialists committed to creating certified reinforcement materials with consistency, precision and care. Based in Kaçanik, Western Balkans, we combine textile engineering, materials science and construction expertise into a single, fully-integrated production facility — from yarn to finished roll.

Our Mission
/

To manufacture ETA-assessed and CE-marked fiberglass reinforcement mesh through a controlled in-house production process—supporting energy-efficient construction, durable façades and reliable supply across Europe. We empower our partners with reliability, responsiveness and long-term collaboration.
