Our History

Guided by precision, reliability, and responsibility, GATEX continues to strengthen the future of construction—transforming raw materials into durable systems that build trust over time.

2016

The Foundation

GATEX was established in Kaçanik, Kosovo, starting with the coating and finishing of glass fibre mesh on its first coating line; a second coating line followed in 2018.

2019

Weaving Launch

In November 2019 GATEX installed its first 16 weaving machines and began weaving its own mesh from glass fibre yarn and roving, completing the production cycle from warping and weaving to coating and finishing.

2020

Certification Milestones

We achieved TÜV verification and secured ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certifications. That same year, GATEX became ETAG 004 compliant and earned CE marking, establishing a strong foundation for European market expansion.

2022

REACH Compliance & Climate Responsibility

GATEX strengthened its REACH compliance framework and introduced its internal Climate Neutral commitment, reinforcing the company’s long-term focus on product safety, environmental responsibility and more sustainable manufacturing practices.

2024–2025

ETA Certification & Capacity Expansion

GATEX reached an important technical milestone with European Technical Assessment ETA-24/0211 issued by ZAG, while expanding production capacity and securing new space for the company’s future industrial development.

2026

Innovation & Expansion

We plan to introduce new mesh variants tailored for specialized insulation systems and expand our logistics capacity and partnership network across Central and Southern Europe—continuing to build on our values of certified quality, regional strength, and production precision.
