Sustainability at Gatex:

Acting locally, building responsibly.
At GATEX, sustainability begins with how we manufacture. Our focus is on reducing unnecessary resource use, improving production efficiency and continuously strengthening the environmental performance of our fiberglass reinforcement mesh operations.

Lower Emissions in Production
Our manufacturing operations rely on grid electricity and LPG, with a continued focus on efficient energy use, process optimisation and responsible resource management.
Through ongoing monitoring and operational improvements, we work to reduce unnecessary energy consumption while maintaining consistent production quality and reliability.
Responsible Material Choices
We work with suppliers to select glass fibre yarns, coatings and other production inputs with documented technical and chemical compliance. Our approach focuses on material efficiency, responsible sourcing and packaging improvements that reduce unnecessary material use across production and distribution.

Supporting Energy-Efficient Construction
GATEX reinforcement mesh is designed for use within ETICS façade systems, where it supports the durability, stability and long-term performance of the insulation layer. By contributing to reliable façade systems, our products form part of construction solutions designed to improve building energy performance.
Our Sustainability Commitments
GATEX operates an ISO 14001-certified environmental management system and is committed to continuous improvement through responsible resource use, process efficiency and structured environmental management.
Qualifying reinforcement mesh products are supported by European Technical Assessment, corresponding Declarations of Performance and CE marking.

Climate Responsibility at Gatex:

Local action. Long-term responsibility.
For GATEX, climate responsibility is not a separate initiative from manufacturing — it is part of how we improve the process itself. Our approach focuses on energy efficiency, better use of materials, responsible production practices and long-term operational improvement.
Responsible Energy Use
Our production facility uses grid electricity and LPG. We continue to improve energy efficiency through process monitoring, equipment optimisation and more efficient production planning, with the aim of reducing unnecessary energy use without compromising product quality.

Material Efficiency
From warping and weaving to coating, curing and finishing, material efficiency is built into the production process. Better process control helps reduce unnecessary material use, improve production consistency and make more efficient use of raw materials across each manufacturing stage.

Supporting Better Façade Performance
Our fiberglass reinforcement mesh is developed for ETICS and related façade applications, supporting crack resistance, dimensional stability and long-term system performance. In this way, GATEX contributes to façade solutions designed for durable and energy-efficient buildings.
Our Environmental Commitment
GATEX is committed to continuous environmental improvement under ISO 14001, with a focus on responsible resource use, process efficiency and long-term reduction of the environmental impact of our manufacturing activities.



Safety & Well-Being at Gatex:

Putting people, process and quality first.
At GATEX, safe manufacturing and consistent product quality are closely connected. Our approach combines structured workplace procedures, trained personnel, responsible materials handling and continuous process control throughout production.
Safe Production Practices
Our manufacturing operations follow documented procedures for machinery operation, materials handling and production control. Safety measures are integrated across warping, weaving, coating, curing, finishing and packaging activities.

Workplace Safety & Employee Care
GATEX operates an ISO 45001-certified occupational health and safety management system. Training, protective equipment, structured workflows and regular internal review support safe working conditions and continuous improvement across the production environment.

Product & Material Compliance
Technical and chemical information is maintained for relevant raw materials and production inputs, while product-specific documentation supports correct handling, storage, installation and use. Qualifying reinforcement mesh products are supported by applicable technical and regulatory documentation.

Continuous Risk Management
Workplace risks, production procedures and operational controls are reviewed as part of our ongoing safety management process. Clear communication, defined responsibilities and continuous improvement help maintain a safe and professional working environment across the facility.

Our Commitment in Action: We focus on safe work, controlled production and clear technical communication — because better products begin with better processes.
