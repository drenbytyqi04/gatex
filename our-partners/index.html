Our Partners

We take pride in collaborating with trusted distributors, construction specialists, and system manufacturers across Europe. Each partnership is built on mutual reliability, shared standards of quality, and a long-term commitment to advancing façade and insulation technologies together.

Built on consistency.

‍Grown through trust.

At GATEX, we see partnership as an extension of the way we manufacture: it has to be consistent, reliable, and built to last. The same attention we give to product quality, process control, and technical detail also defines the way we work with the people and companies around us. For us, a strong partnership is not measured only by the number of orders placed, but by the confidence that develops over time—through clear communication, dependable service and the ability to deliver with the same level of care, again and again.We work with suppliers to select glass fibre yarns, coatings and other production inputs with documented technical and chemical compliance. Our approach focuses on material efficiency, responsible sourcing and packaging improvements that reduce unnecessary material use across production and distribution.
Our aim is to create long-term relationships with partners who value quality, responsiveness and continuous improvement. Whether the cooperation begins with raw-material supply, technical development, private-label production, distribution or market expansion, we approach each relationship as part of a broader industrial ecosystem. We listen carefully to requirements, adapt where flexibility is needed, and work to maintain consistency in product, documentation, delivery, and support.
We believe that sustainable growth comes from mutual reliability. Our partners need to know that the product they receive today will meet the same expectations tomorrow, that communication remains direct when challenges arise, and that growth does not come at the expense of quality. In return, long-standing partnerships help us understand markets better, improve our processes and develop products that respond more accurately to real customer needs.
This philosophy allows GATEX to grow not simply as a manufacturer, but as a dependable part of a wider European supply chain—connecting production, technical knowledge, distribution and long-term commercial relationships. Because for us, building better means creating value that remains consistent in the product, in the process, and in the partnership itself.
