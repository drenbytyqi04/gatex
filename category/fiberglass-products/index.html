Fiberglass Products

At GATEX, fiberglass reinforcement mesh is at the core of what we do—combining strength, flexibility, and durability for modern External Thermal Insulation Composite Systems (ETICS) and façade applications. Our product range is developed to support reliable reinforcement, dimensional stability, and efficient installation across a wide variety of construction requirements. Available in different weights, mesh configurations, colours, and roll formats, GATEX fiberglass mesh is designed to meet both standard and demanding façade applications. Through precision manufacturing, continuous quality control and ongoing product development, we deliver reinforcement solutions that combine consistent performance, workability and long-term reliability.
